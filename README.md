# KastaCD

**KastaCD** is an all-in-one party addon built for Mythic+ and PvP on this Legion 7.3.5 server. Instead of juggling five different addons to know your group's cooldowns, who can interrupt what, which mob to focus, and whether your key is announced — KastaCD does all of it in one place, already set up to work the moment you install it.

---

## Why KastaCD?

- **One addon, not five.** Cooldowns, interrupts, CC, keystone tools, nameplate coloring, and more — no need to stitch together separate addons that don't talk to each other.
- **Works out of the box.** Spells, dungeon data, and nameplate colors are already loaded in. You don't have to configure anything to get useful information on your first pull.
- **Built for this server specifically**, not a generic retail port — every feature has been tuned and bug-fixed against how Legion actually behaves here.
- **Plays nicely with your UI.** Whether you use default nameplates, TidyPlates, Plater, or ElvUI, KastaCD adapts to it instead of fighting it.
- **Actually maintained.** Bugs reported get fixed fast, and features get refined based on real feedback from real pulls — not left half-finished.

---

## Features

### Party Cooldowns
See your whole group's important cooldowns at a glance — defensives, immunities, interrupts, and offensive cooldowns — as icons anchored right next to each party member's frame. Pick exactly which spells you care about, per class. Icons automatically account for what spec someone actually is, so you're never shown a cooldown they don't have.

### Interrupt Tracker & Crowd Control Tracker
Two dedicated bars showing who can interrupt and who has stuns/roots/CC available, and when each comes off cooldown. No more asking "who has a kick?" mid-pull.

### Keystone Helper
A small toolkit for Mythic+ runs:
- **Ready Check** and **Pull Timer** buttons added right onto the keystone frame.
- Automatically inserts your keystone when you open the font of power.
- Shows each mob's contribution to the dungeon's "enemy forces" percentage directly on its nameplate.

### Key Announcer
Ever had nobody share their keystone at the start of a run? Type **"keys please"** in party/raid/guild chat and KastaCD automatically announces your keystone's dungeon and level back — no more awkward silence while everyone digs through their bags.

### KastaPlates
Color specific enemies' nameplates so the important ones stand out — kick targets, CC targets, whatever you decide matters. Comes with a curated list of dangerous mobs already colored for you across every Legion Mythic+ dungeon, with a full 3D model preview so you know exactly what you're looking for before you even see it. Works with default nameplates, TidyPlates, Plater, and ElvUI alike.

### Affix Call-outs
Warns you out loud when an Explosive Orb spawns or Quaking is about to hit, so you don't get caught out mid-fight.

### Personal Leaderboard
Automatically records your own best completion time for every dungeon, so you can track your own improvement over time.

### Overshield Display
Shows shield amounts that go past a player's max health, instead of hiding the extra absorb.

### Profiles
Create, switch between, and delete named profiles. Export a profile as a copy-paste string, or post it straight to chat so a friend can import your whole setup with one command.

### Quality of Life
- Party Cooldown icons automatically disappear for anyone who goes offline, and reappear the moment they're back.
- Settings are content-aware — choose whether tracking is active in Open World, Dungeons, Arena, or Battlegrounds.
- Hovering any spell shows its cooldown, duration, spec requirement, and level.

## Slash Commands

| Command | Description |
|---|---|
| `/kcd`, `/kastacd`, `/kasta` | Open the settings menu |
| `/kcdkeys` | Announce your own keystone (`/kcdkeys ask` to prompt others, `/kcdkeys test` to preview) |
| `/kcdimport <name>` | Import a profile someone shared with you in chat |
| `/kcdboard` | View your personal Mythic+ best times |
| `/rl` | Quick `/reload` shortcut |
